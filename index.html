Relay: reliable webhook delivery service

Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL

Accepts an event once and guarantees every subscribed endpoint eventually receives it. Events and deliveries are written in one transaction, workers claim jobs with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED and leases, failures retry with jittered backoff, and idempotency keys and a dead-letter queue with replay cover the rest.

  • 44 tests on real PostgreSQL
  • ~1,000 deliveries/s on 2 CPUs
  • 0 double deliveries with 4 concurrent workers
